Privacy policy – Lasius

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Français

Lasius is a messaging app that works without the internet: messages go directly from one device to another over Bluetooth. We don't run any server. Nothing reaches us, except the reports you choose to send us.

1. Data collected

Lasius doesn't send anything to our servers or to third parties. The app contains no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and creates no account. The only exception is a report, which you send yourself by email (section 4).

2. Data stored on your device

  • The nickname you choose and a random identifier for the device.
  • The history of your conversations and your encryption keys.
  • Messages waiting to be delivered or relayed for other users (kept for 6 hours at most).
  • The relays through which your messages best reach each of your contacts; these trails fade away on their own over time.
  • The list of contacts you have blocked: their nickname and what is needed to recognize their device.

This data stays on your device. The history, waiting messages and relay trails are encrypted, and the keys are kept in the system's secure keychain. You can erase them by deleting a conversation, unblocking a contact or uninstalling the app.

3. Data exchanged with nearby devices

Lasius connects on its own to nearby Lasius devices to pass messages along. With an unknown device, the link stays anonymous: it receives neither your nickname nor your identifier, only a pseudonym drawn at random for that link. What your device broadcasts over Bluetooth changes regularly, and only your contacts can recognize it.

Your nickname, your identifier and your public key only go to your contacts, and to the people you choose to introduce yourself to.

Messages are end-to-end encrypted: only the recipient can read them. A device acting as a relay can neither read nor modify a message, and knows neither who wrote it nor who it is for; it only sees when it passes through and its approximate size.

4. Reports

When you report a contact or a message, Lasius prepares an email to us in your email app. It contains the contact's nickname and Lasius identifier and, if you report a message, that message and its date. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself, and you can edit the draft.

We use these reports only to handle the reported abuse, and we delete them within one year at the latest. The email goes through your email provider, under its own rules.

5. Permissions

Lasius asks for Bluetooth access to find nearby devices and exchange messages. On older versions of Android (11 and below), the system also requires the location permission for Bluetooth; Lasius doesn't use your location and doesn't store it.

6. Children

Apart from the reports you choose to send, Lasius collects no personal data, including children's data.

7. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published, with a new update date.
